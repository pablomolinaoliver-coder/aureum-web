Social Media

How an insurance brokerage generates leads with social content

By Aureum Digital Studio·5 min read

Insurance sells on trust, and trust today is built on social media. A brokerage that posts strategically turns frequent questions into qualified clients, week after week.

Your client has questions: answer them first

«What does home insurance cover?», «Is comprehensive cover worth it for me?». Every frequent question is a content idea. If you answer before anyone else, you position yourself as the go-to expert.

Educational content generates 3 times more leads than traditional advertising, at a fraction of the cost.

A content plan that sells without selling

  • Educational: explain coverage and answer real questions in plain language.
  • Real cases: situations where insurance saved a client.
  • Closeness: put a face to the team; insurance is bought from people.
  • Call to action: every piece invites a no-commitment quote.

Turn reach into leads

Posting isn't enough: you have to capture the prospect. We connect your social media management with an AI assistant that replies to messages instantly, qualifies and books the call. That way no prospect goes cold.

Measure to improve

Each month we review which content generates the most contacts and double down on what works.
