Local SEO for restaurants: how to fill tables from Google

By Aureum Digital Studio·6 min read

When someone searches «where to eat near me», Google decides which three restaurants it shows first. Being in that local top 3 fills tables; staying out empties them. Local SEO is what makes the difference.

The map rules: Google's local pack

Before the website, the first thing a hungry customer sees is the map block with three businesses. Appearing there depends mostly on your Google Business profile: how close you are, how complete your profile is and how good your review history is.

76% of people searching for something nearby on mobile visit a business within 24 hours. Purchase intent is extremely high.

Optimize your profile in detail

  • Categoría correcta (restaurante, maríspería, pizzería…) y atributos (terraza, reservas, sin gluten).
  • Appetizing, up-to-date photos of your dishes and venue.
  • Hours and menu always current, holidays included.
  • Weekly posts with news, events or the daily menu.

Reviews are your best card

Ask for reviews systematically (a QR code on the table or an automatic message after the visit) and reply to all of them, including the negative ones. A steady, well-managed flow of reviews raises your ranking and your credibility at the same time.

Back the map with your website

A fast website with your menu, photos and a booking button reinforces your local SEO and turns visits into diners.
