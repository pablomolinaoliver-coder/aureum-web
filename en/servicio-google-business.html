Why it works, with data.

«Near me» searches end in a visit

76% of people who search for something nearby from their phone visit a business within 24 hours, and 28% of those searches end in a purchase. Whoever shows first on the map takes that visit. Source: Google data compiled in 15 Google My Business statistics

Photos bring clients, literally

Profiles with photos get 42% more «directions» requests and 35% more clicks to the website than profiles without photos. That's why we care for and refresh them every month. Source: Google data compiled in Google Maps and Google My Business statistics

Google is where reviews are read

83% of consumers use Google to check reviews of local businesses, well ahead of any other platform. And only 4% say they never read them. Your profile is your most-seen shop window. Source: BrightLocal, Local Consumer Review Survey 2025

The profile answers before your website

64% of consumers use the Google Business profile to find a business's phone or address. If it has incomplete or outdated data, that call goes to your competition. Source: data compiled in 15 Google My Business statistics
