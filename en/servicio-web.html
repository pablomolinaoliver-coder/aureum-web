What a website brings your business, with data.

No empty promises: here's what the studies say.

Your clients research online before deciding

80% of consumers research online before buying, even when the purchase happens in a physical store. If you're not there, the comparison is won by those who are. Source: informed consumer study (Google/Bluecaribu)

In hospitality, the website decides bookings

77% of diners check a restaurant's website before visiting and 93% look at the menu online before deciding. And nearly 70% have ruled out a restaurant just because of its website. Sources: Restaurant Dive · MGH survey (PR Newswire)

Speed is not a technical whim

According to Google, 53% of users abandon a mobile site if it takes more than 3 seconds to load, and each extra second cuts conversions by up to 20%. That's why we build ultra-light websites, with no heavy templates. Source: Think with Google

Mobile rules in Spain

More than 85% of internet traffic in Spain is on mobile. All our websites are designed mobile-first, then for desktop, not the other way around. Source: Semrush / IT User

FAQ

We answer your questions

Which website is right for me?

If you need clear presence and direct contact (restaurant, trade, clinic), the Essential. If you want to rank and capture leads with several pages and a blog, the Professional. If your client is international or you need bookings, the Premium. In the free audit we tell you straight.

How long until my website is ready?

The Essential is usually published in under 48 hours from receiving your content. The Premium, 2 to 3 weeks.

What is the difference between the price with and without a plan?

With any monthly plan (minimum 6 months) we reduce the website build price by €100. Without a plan you pay the full fee once, with no commitment.

What if I don't contract any monthly plan?

The website is yours anyway: we deliver it published, with first-year domain and hosting included. Plans only add maintenance, changes and continuous visibility.

Does it include copy and images?

Yes, we write conversion-focused copy from your information. Images can be yours, professional stock or custom-generated.

What happens after the first year of domain and hosting?

Renewal costs around €15-30 a year and we notify you in advance. With any monthly plan, we manage it and it's included.

Shall we talk about your new website?

Tell us what you need and we'll send a tailored proposal within 24 hours.
